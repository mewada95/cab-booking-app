Terms & Conditions

1. Partial Payment Terms

Advance booking is ₹0. Customer must pay 20% of total fare directly to the driver at trip origin (start point), and remaining 80% upon arrival at dropoff.

2. Tolls, Taxes & Parking

All highway tolls, state border permit taxes, and parking fees are strictly NOT included in estimated fare. They must be paid as actuals during the journey.

3. Night Driving Allowance

A driver night allowance of ₹250 applies if travel extends between 10:00 PM and 06:00 AM.

4. Free Cancellation

Zero charges if canceled at least 2 hours before pickup. Standard minimum fee applies if cab has reached pickup point.
